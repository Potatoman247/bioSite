The subject of this project is Madi Barr. Madi and I have been together for just over four years now. She truly is the greatest thing that has ever happened to me. Madi grew up doing musical theater, participating in The Music Room from age 10 to age 17. In high school, Madi participated in our school’s Color Guard, which is actually how we first began to meet. Since graduating, She began working at Disneyland as a Gatherer at Savi’s Workshop, the Lightsaber Building Experience. She loves to read, and can often speed through a full major novel in under a day! Lately, she has begun getting into watching football with me, and she really seems to love it! What started out as a thing on the side that I engaged with on my own has blossomed into something that we both can really dive into and enjoy together! She grew up a San Diego Chargers fan, but after they moved to LA her family became a San Fransisco 49ers fan. My family loves the niners, so it was very easy for her to grow to be a fan of them, though she will always have the Chargers on the top for her. I am very happy to be able to make this project about her, because I want to show just how incredible she truly is!
